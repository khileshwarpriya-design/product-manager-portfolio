Problem

Tenant onboarding and HRIS configuration can involve repetitive setup steps, manual knowledge retrieval, and coordination across teams.

Product approach

  • Delivered an AI-powered Admin Assistant using LLMs and retrieval-augmented generation, as described in the resume.
  • Focused on shortening the path from configuration intent to completion.

How I think about AI product quality

AI is valuable when it reliably helps a user complete a meaningful task—not simply when a model produces a fluent answer. My product lens includes task success, time saved, factual grounding, user trust, privacy, escalation paths, and operational cost.

My product approach

From user problem to measurable outcome

A practical approach for AI and platform products: start with the job to be done, validate the workflow, then evaluate the outcome and its risks.

01 / DISCOVER

Understand the user

Interview users and stakeholders, map the workflow, inspect support themes, and establish the baseline.

02 / DEFINE

Choose the right problem

Set a clear problem statement, success metric, non-goals, and a prioritized MVP scope.

03 / DELIVER

Build with guardrails

Partner with design and engineering on requirements, testing, rollout, permissions, and failure handling.

04 / LEARN

Measure and iterate

Track adoption, task success, quality, time saved, customer feedback, and unintended outcomes.

AI-specific considerations

  • Ground responses in trusted context; make uncertainty visible.
  • Use human approval for consequential or irreversible actions.
  • Measure quality and failure cases, not just response speed.
  • Protect customer data and respect role-based permissions.

Product management strengths

  • Product requirements, user stories, backlog prioritization, and roadmap planning.
  • Cross-functional execution with engineering, design, QA, and business teams.
  • API and platform product thinking with enterprise constraints.
  • Analytics-led decisions, customer feedback, launch enablement, and GTM.
